These projects matter because they shape the same evaluation conversation, even when they are broader than Ezkey, more enterprise-centered, or more specialized.

They help answer the practical question: if Ezkey is not a full IdP, not a provisioning suite, and not just an authenticator app, what sits around it in the real open-source identity and MFA landscape?

## What this category clarifies for Ezkey

These projects matter less as direct product matches than as boundary markers. They show where Ezkey stops being a close comparator and starts touching adjacent needs: MFA server breadth, identity governance, federation, or broader IAM and CIAM scope.

That distinction is useful for readers. It helps explain why Ezkey may still enter the same evaluation conversation, while remaining a narrower and much earlier product with a different center of gravity.

## Notes

- privacyIDEA is the most credible pure MFA-server comparator and is EU-based. It is narrower than a full auth platform, while Ezkey couples the MFA backend with a first-party mobile app and Admin UI.
- WSO2 and Casdoor are general-purpose IdPs; broader and more SSO-centered than Ezkey.
- Apache Syncope and OpenIAM are governance and provisioning suites where MFA is only one part of a larger platform.
- StrongKey brings real crypto and PKI depth, but reads more like a toolkit than a turnkey MFA product.
- Shibboleth is a federation IdP, which is a different problem from Ezkey's MFA trust boundary.
