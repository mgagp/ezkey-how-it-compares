## Positioning

Converged Identity Governance and Administration and Access Management platform for regulated or enterprise-heavy environments. It is useful as a landscape reference, but it is far from Ezkey's narrow MFA thesis.

## Deployment

On-premises or cloud deployment models, with enterprise-oriented delivery assets such as Terraform, Helm, RPM, Kubernetes, and OpenShift support.

## MFA and strong auth

- Passwordless authentication
- Adaptive authentication
- SSO
- Social authentication
- Self-service portal

## Protocols and trust model

Provisioning and de-provisioning connectors, access reviews, lifecycle automation, and compliance-oriented identity controls. The posture is governance and enterprise-suite centric.

## Admin, API, and mobile posture

Enterprise administration and governance workflows with a heavy IGA orientation. MFA is present, but not as the product core.

## Where it sits versus Ezkey

OpenIAM is the furthest of the adjacent set from Ezkey's thesis. It is best treated as an enterprise IAM and governance reference rather than a Duo-like or backend-first MFA comparator.
