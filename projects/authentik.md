## Positioning

Self-hosted identity provider positioned as a replacement for hosted IdPs, with large adoption and strong emphasis on keeping critical authentication infrastructure and sensitive data on your own systems.

## Deployment

Docker Compose, Kubernetes, and Terraform, with prebuilt workflow templates and infrastructure-as-code.

## MFA and strong auth

- MFA with multiple second factors
- WebAuthn and passkeys
- Conditional access
- Enrollment and self-service flows
- Configurable multi-stage authentication

## Protocols and trust model

OAuth2, OIDC, SAML2, LDAP, RADIUS, SCIM, SSF, and Kerberos, with application proxy and federation support. The posture is general-purpose IdP and browser or SSO centric.

## Admin, API, and mobile posture

Strong admin UI, comprehensive REST API, and a large integration catalog. No dedicated device-cryptographic mobile app.

## Where it sits versus Ezkey

One of the strongest modern EU comparators and an excellent self-hosted IdP. It is a full general-purpose identity provider, whereas Ezkey deliberately narrows scope to cryptographic, mobile-assisted MFA and does not aim to replace an IdP or SSO hub.
