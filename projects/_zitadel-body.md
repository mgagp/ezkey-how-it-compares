## Positioning

API-first, multi-tenant identity infrastructure designed to let teams build authentication directly into their applications. It bridges open-source and enterprise identity expectations.

## Deployment

Self-hostable across regions or fully managed as SaaS. SDK examples exist across several stacks, including Go, Angular, React, Next.js, Flutter, and Python.

## MFA and strong auth

- MFA with configurable second factors
- Passkeys and passwordless flows
- Per-organization second-factor enforcement
- Hosted login page or build-your-own login UI

## Protocols and trust model

OIDC, SAML, gRPC and REST APIs, RBAC, multi-tenant organizations, and event-driven actions. The posture is identity infrastructure: cloud- and API-first.

## Admin, API, and mobile posture

Excellent API-first design with strong admin capabilities. A Flutter SDK example demonstrates mobile integration, though there is no first-party cryptographic device app.

## Where it sits versus Ezkey

Zitadel shares Ezkey's API-first DNA and multi-tenant framing most closely of any candidate. It is broader identity infrastructure; Ezkey concentrates specifically on the MFA trust boundary and cryptographic continuity rather than full IAM.
