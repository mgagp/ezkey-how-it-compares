## Positioning

Open-source identity management platform with an enterprise-oriented focus on digital identity, provisioning, and lifecycle control. It is relevant to Ezkey mainly as part of the broader IAM and IGA landscape rather than as a direct MFA product comparator.

## Deployment

Self-hosted enterprise platform with a strong server-side administration and integration posture.

## MFA and strong auth

- Access management capabilities
- MFA primarily via integration patterns
- Stronger orientation toward identity governance than toward MFA as a product core

## Protocols and trust model

Identity management, provisioning, LDAP and Active Directory integration, and SSO patterns. The trust posture is governance and provisioning centric, not centered on a mobile-backed cryptographic MFA chain.

## Admin, API, and mobile posture

Good REST API and enterprise-oriented administration surface. MFA is not the primary product thesis, and there is no first-party mobile participant comparable to Ezkey's app.

## Where it sits versus Ezkey

Apache Syncope is useful as an IAM and governance reference, but the center of gravity is different. Ezkey is authentication and MFA centric, not an IGA or provisioning suite.
