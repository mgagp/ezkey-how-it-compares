## Positioning

Open-source enterprise identity platform positioned for customer identity and access management and broader IAM use cases. It sits in the same broad decision set as other self-hosted identity suites, but with a heavier enterprise footprint than Ezkey.

## Deployment

Self-hosted platform intended for enterprise deployment and operations, with commercial support and adjacent managed offerings in the wider WSO2 ecosystem.

## MFA and strong auth

- MFA
- SSO
- Adaptive authentication
- OTP-based factors

## Protocols and trust model

OpenID Connect, SAML, OAuth2, federation, and enterprise CIAM patterns. The posture is standards-rich and enterprise-centered rather than narrowly focused on a backend-first cryptographic MFA flow.

## Admin, API, and mobile posture

Strong enterprise administration surface and API support, but with a heavier operational footprint and broader configuration surface than Ezkey.

## Where it sits versus Ezkey

WSO2 Identity Server is a strong benchmark for mature self-hosted enterprise identity, but it is broader and heavier than Ezkey's intended scope. Ezkey aims for a narrower 80/20 MFA product with more constrained operational shape.
